Your Guide to Journaling

🌸 Welcome to your little space, just for you. There is no right or wrong way to journal. Write a little or a lot, whenever it feels helpful. Every page is part of your journey.

✍️ Creating a New Entry

From the main menu, choose New Entry. Take your time to write about your day, your thoughts, or anything on your mind.

  • Choose the emotions that describe how you're feeling.
  • Record any exercise or activities you'd like to remember.
  • Add photographs to keep those moments close.
  • Tap the star (☆) to mark a meaningful day. You can also write what made that day special, and revisit it in your journal later.
  • Save your entry when you're ready. You can revisit it later.

📖 Revisiting Previous Entries

Choose Previous Entries from the main menu to look back at what you've written. Open an entry to read it in full, revisit your selections and see its photographs.

Tap a photograph to open Photos & Memories. Swipe left or right to explore more photographs from that entry, then tap the enlarged photo to return.

💗 Exploring My Emotions

Choose Emotions to reflect on how you've been feeling over time. Explore the available emotions and view the graphs. Tap a circular data point on the graph to display the journal entry or entries for that point underneath the graph. You can then open an entry to revisit the moments behind it.

There is no need to judge how you feel. Your emotions are simply part of your story.

🌿 Exploring My Exercise

Choose Exercise to revisit the activities you've recorded. Your charts can help you spot patterns. Tap a circular data point on the graph to display the journal entry or entries for that point underneath the graph. Open an entry to see what was happening on those days.

🌸 Making Journal Your Own

Open Settings → My Emotions & Exercise to add your own choices, each with a single emoji. Hide choices you don't use, or remove ones you've added yourself.

Previously recorded emotions and activities are kept in your entries and charts, even when you hide or remove a choice.

🖼️ Personalizing Your Homepage

In Settings → Personalize My Journal, choose between rotating pastel artwork and your own photograph. If you use a photograph, adjust its zoom and position until it feels just right.

Your homepage photograph is stored on this device, so keep a separate copy of the original.

🔒 Keeping Your Memories Safe

Your entries and photographs are stored locally on the device and browser you're using. Journal doesn't automatically synchronize them between devices.

  • Backup My Journal: download a password-encrypted copy of your entries and photographs. Keep both the file and its password safe.
  • Restore My Journal: choose a previous backup and enter its password to recover your saved memories.
  • ✧ Backup Reminder: opt in to a gentle reminder about every 30 days when you open Journal.
  • Journal PIN: set a six-digit lock and recovery question to help keep the app screen private on this device.

The PIN protects the app screen, not the underlying browser storage. The encrypted backup password is separate from your PIN.

💛 A Gentle Reminder

You don't have to journal every day, find the perfect words, or feel a certain way. Some days you might write pages; other days, just a sentence. Journal is here whenever you need somewhere to pause, reflect and unpack what's on your mind.
