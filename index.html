Settings
✧ ♡ ✧

Keep Your Memories Safe

Your memories deserve a safe place to stay.

💛 Personalize My Journal

Enjoy a different pastel illustration whenever Journal starts, or choose a personal photograph that stays until you change it.

Your chosen homepage photograph is stored on this device. It is not included in the existing entry backup, so please keep the original in your Gallery.

↧ Backup My Journal

Save your entries, emotions, activities and photographs in one password-encrypted backup file. Keep the password somewhere safe; it cannot be recovered.

↥ Restore My Journal

Restore entries and photos from a backup. Existing entries with different IDs are kept; matching IDs are updated only after confirmation.

♧ Backup Reminder

A gentle reminder when it's time to save another copy.

🔒 Journal PIN

Protect Journal with a six-digit PIN when opening the app.

Your journal remains stored on this device. A PIN protects the app screen, not the underlying browser database. Backup files are encrypted separately with a password.
