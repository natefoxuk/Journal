Your Guide to Journaling

🌸 Welcome to your little space, just for you. There is no right or wrong way to journal. Write a little or a lot, whenever it feels helpful. Every page is part of your journey.

✍️ Creating a New Entry

From the main menu, choose New Entry. Take your time to write about your day, your thoughts, or anything on your mind.

  • Choose the emotions that describe how you're feeling.
  • Record any activities you'd like to remember.
  • Add photographs to keep those moments close.
  • Tap the star (☆) to mark a meaningful day. You can also write what made that day special, and revisit it in your journal later.
  • Save your entry when you're ready. You can revisit it later.

📖 Revisiting Previous Entries

Choose Previous Entries from the main menu and select a date. Tap Open Full Entry to read your memory on a dedicated journal page, including your emotion, activity, written thoughts, special moment and photographs. Tap Edit to change the entry using the familiar form; saving returns to the reading page.

When you add a photo, you can optionally write a short caption. Tap a photograph to open Photos & Memories, where the entry date appears above your photographs. Swipe left or right to explore more photographs from that entry. A caption appears beneath each photo, or you can tap the pencil to add or edit one. Tap the enlarged photo to return.

💗 Exploring My Emotions

Choose Emotions to reflect on how you've been feeling over time. Explore the available emotions and view the graphs. Tap a circular data point on the graph to display the journal entry or entries for that point underneath the graph. You can then open an entry to revisit the moments behind it.

There is no need to judge how you feel. Your emotions are simply part of your story.

🌿 Exploring My Activity

Choose Activity to revisit the activities you've recorded. Your charts can help you spot patterns. Tap a circular data point on the graph to display the journal entry or entries for that point underneath the graph. Open an entry to see what was happening on those days.

🌸 Making Journal Your Own

Open Settings → Personalize My Journal → Emotions & Activity Personalization to add your own choices, each with a single emoji. Hide choices you don't use, or remove ones you've added yourself.

Your past entries remain intact when you hide a choice. Hidden choices disappear from insights until you show them again.

🖼️ Personalizing Your Homepage

In Settings → Personalize My Journal → Homepage Appearance, choose between rotating pastel artwork and your own photograph. If you use a photograph, adjust its zoom and position until it feels just right.

Your homepage photograph is stored on this device, so keep a separate copy of the original.

💛 Accessibility

In Settings → Personalize My Journal → Accessibility, choose Small, Medium or Large text, enable gentle button sounds, turn on high contrast, make touch targets larger or reduce movement. You can also switch Read My Entry Aloud on or off.

When enabled, a read-aloud button appears beneath your written thoughts and beneath the special-moment text box when you tap the star. You can listen to either section separately, or tap Stop Reading to stop. Photo captions also have a Read Caption Aloud button beneath the caption when this setting is enabled. Speech availability depends on your device and browser.

🔒 Keeping Your Memories Safe

Open Settings → Privacy & Security to find your PIN, backup, restore and reminder options.

Your entries and photographs are stored locally on the device and browser you're using. Journal doesn't automatically synchronize them between devices.

  • Privacy & Security → Backup My Journal: download a password-encrypted copy of your entries and photographs. Keep both the file and its password safe.
  • Restore My Journal: choose a previous backup and enter its password to recover your saved memories.
  • ✧ Backup Reminder: opt in to a gentle reminder about every 30 days when you open Journal.
  • Privacy & Security → Journal PIN: set a six-digit lock and recovery question to help keep the app screen private on this device.

The PIN protects the app screen, not the underlying browser storage. The encrypted backup password is separate from your PIN.

💛 A Gentle Reminder

You don't have to journal every day, find the perfect words, or feel a certain way. Some days you might write pages; other days, just a sentence. Journal is here whenever you need somewhere to pause, reflect and unpack what's on your mind.

Journal

A space for every part of your journey

💛

Journal was created with a simple belief: everyone deserves a quiet, personal space to express their thoughts, understand their emotions, and reflect on life's experiences.

Whether you're celebrating a beautiful moment, working through a difficult day, or simply taking time for yourself, Journal is here to help you capture what matters.

There's no right or wrong way to journal, no expectations, and no pressure. Just a place to be yourself, one entry at a time.
